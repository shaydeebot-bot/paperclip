# Competitive Brief

A concise, structured document that captures the competitive landscape for a specific product, market, or feature area. Designed for handoff to spec writers, marketing, or the founding team.

## Brief Structure

### 1. Market Overview
- Market size and growth rate (cite source)
- Key buyer segments
- Dominant distribution channels
- 1-2 macro trends shaping the market

### 2. Competitor Profiles (3-5 key players)

For each competitor:

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Company** | Name + URL |
| **Founded / Funding** | Stage and notable investors |
| **Target customer** | Primary ICP |
| **Core product** | What it does in one sentence |
| **Pricing** | Model + entry price |
| **Key differentiator** | Their primary claim |
| **Weakness** | Most cited complaint (G2, Reddit, reviews) |
| **Distribution** | How they acquire customers |

### 3. Positioning Map

2x2 matrix comparing the two most important axes for this market (e.g. Price vs. Ease of Use, Breadth vs. Depth). Place each competitor and our product on the map.

### 4. Messaging Analysis
- What pain do competitors lead with?
- What outcomes do they promise?
- What words and phrases dominate their homepages and ads?

### 5. Gaps & Opportunities
- Underserved segments
- Features commonly requested but not delivered
- Pricing gaps
- Channels competitors are ignoring

### 6. Strategic Recommendations
- 3 actionable positioning moves
- 1 messaging angle to avoid (already crowded)
- 1 differentiator to double down on

## Sources to Check
- Competitor websites and pricing pages
- G2, Capterra, Trustpilot reviews
- Reddit (r/[category], search competitor name)
- ProductHunt launches and comments
- LinkedIn job postings (signals priorities)
- SEMrush / SimilarWeb traffic data
- Crunchbase for funding signals
