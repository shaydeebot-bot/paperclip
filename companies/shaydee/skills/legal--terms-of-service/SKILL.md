# Terms of Service Generator

Generate comprehensive terms of service tailored to the actual product's features and business model.

## When to Use

When generating or reviewing terms of service. Always read the codebase to understand the product's features, user roles, and business model.

## Process

1. **Understand the product** — What does it do? Who are the user types? Is there user-generated content? Payments? Marketplace dynamics?
2. **Identify user actions** — What can users do? Create accounts, submit content, make purchases, generate links, etc.
3. **Identify risks** — What could go wrong? Fraud, misuse, data loss, service interruptions
4. **Generate ToS** — Write terms covering all the above

## Required Sections

1. **Acceptance of Terms** — By using the service, you agree
2. **Description of Service** — What the product does
3. **Account Registration** — Requirements, responsibilities, security
4. **User Responsibilities** — Acceptable use, prohibited activities
5. **Intellectual Property** — Who owns what (platform content, user content, trademarks)
6. **Payment Terms** — If applicable: billing, refunds, cancellation
7. **Limitation of Liability** — Disclaimers, warranty limitations, liability caps
8. **Indemnification** — User indemnifies the platform
9. **Termination** — When accounts can be suspended/terminated
10. **Dispute Resolution** — Governing law, arbitration, jurisdiction
11. **Modifications** — How terms can be updated
12. **Severability** — If one provision is invalid, others remain
13. **Contact Information** — How to reach the company

## Special Considerations by Product Type

### Marketplace/Platform
- Relationship between parties (platform vs sellers vs buyers)
- Commission/fee structure
- Dispute resolution between parties
- Content moderation

### Affiliate/Referral
- FTC disclosure requirements
- Prohibited promotional methods
- Commission clawback conditions
- Brand usage guidelines

### AI/ML Products
- AI-generated content disclaimers
- Accuracy/reliability disclaimers
- User data usage for model training (opt-in/opt-out)

## Rules

- Balance between legal protection and readability
- Be specific to the actual product — not generic
- Mark unknowns with `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]`
- Include "Last updated" and "Effective date"
