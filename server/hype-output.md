## 7. FAQ SECTION

**Q: What files are included in the generated theme?**
A: A complete Shopify theme: `layout/theme.liquid`, templates for index, product, collection, cart, and page types, reusable sections (header, footer, featured collection, hero), `assets/theme.css`, and `config/settings_schema.json`.

**Q: Can I customize the theme after installing it?**
A: Yes. Generated themes use Shopify's standard section architecture. Edit colors, layout, and content directly in the Shopify theme editor — or modify the Liquid code if you want deeper changes.

**Q: Do I need to know how to code?**
A: No. Describe your store, download the ZIP, upload to Shopify. That's it. If you want to tweak the code later, you can — but it's not required.

**Q: Will it work with my Shopify plan?**
A: Yes. Generated themes are standard Shopify themes and work on every Shopify plan — Basic through Plus.

**Q: What happens to my themes if I cancel Pro?**
A: Your themes stay yours. Download them anytime. You just won't be able to generate new ones beyond the free tier limit.

**Q: How is this different from buying a theme on the Shopify Theme Store?**
A: Theme Store themes are one-size-fits-all templates. ThemeForge generates a theme based on your specific inputs — your industry, your color palette, your product types. You get a theme shaped around your store, not a template you reshape around your store.

**Q: Can I sell themes generated with ThemeForge?**
A: Generated themes are yours to use on your own stores. Reselling or distributing generated themes is not permitted under the terms of service.
